✳ THE QUICK ANSWERS

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Ordering, product information and policies.

How do I place an order?

Follow the three steps in our How to Order guide.

Where can I see prices and availability?

Prices, pack sizes, flavours and stock can change. Message the team for the current options, or open the Daraz link on each product for its latest listing.

What about delivery and payment?

For direct orders, confirm delivery to your location, delivery charges and payment options with A1 Nutra Lab before placing an order. For Daraz orders, the available options appear at checkout.

Can I ask for product and label details?

Yes. Ask the team for the current pack size, flavour, ingredient label, batch information and expiry date before ordering.

Does my enquiry bag place an order?

No. It saves products and quantities in this browser. It does not place or pay for an order or reserve stock.

YOUR INFORMATION

PRIVACY & YOUR BAG.

This website lets you browse products and prepare an enquiry on your own device.

THE ENQUIRY BAG.

Your enquiry bag is saved in this browser on this device. It remembers your selected products and quantities, with no name, address or payment information. Clear it with the button below or by clearing this website’s data in your browser settings. If browser storage is unavailable, the bag lasts for the current visit.

MESSAGING AND ORDERS.

Copying an enquiry writes the message to your clipboard when you choose that action. Nothing is sent automatically. Opening Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or Daraz takes you to another service, which has its own privacy practices. The call link opens your device’s calling app. Information you send to the team is handled through that service.

WEBSITE REQUESTS.

This code includes no analytics, advertising tags or tracking cookies. The hosting provider processes requests to serve the site and may apply its own security or request logging. The business’s order-data retention policy and dedicated privacy contact have not been supplied; ask the team on Instagram.
